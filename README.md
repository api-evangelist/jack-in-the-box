# Jack in the Box (jack-in-the-box)

Jack in the Box Inc. (NASDAQ: JACK), founded in 1951 and headquartered in San Diego, California, is a quick-service restaurant company operating and franchising approximately 2,200 Jack in the Box hamburger restaurants across 21 U.S. states. In December 2021 the company acquired Del Taco for $575M; in August 2025 it announced a strategic review of Del Taco and on October 2025 agreed to divest Del Taco (~600 locations) to Yadav Enterprises for $115M, with the transaction closing December 22, 2025. Digital channels include the Jack in the Box mobile app, Jack Pack loyalty program, online ordering, and integrations with third-party delivery platforms (DoorDash, Grubhub, Uber Eats). The company does not publish a public developer API; all digital ordering, loyalty, and franchise systems are operated via internal platforms and partner integrations.

## APIs

Jack in the Box does not publish a public developer API. Its consumer-facing mobile app (the Jack app), Jack Pack loyalty program, online ordering, franchise systems, and third-party delivery integrations are operated through internal platforms and partner integrations rather than an openly documented developer API. The `Jackinthebox-IT` GitHub organization exists but has no public repositories, MCP servers, or Claude Code skills. This profile therefore documents the company, its digital surfaces, and its corporate context rather than a machine-readable API contract.

## Corporate Notes

- **Del Taco Divestiture (2025):** Strategic review announced August 2025; sale to Yadav Enterprises announced October 2025 for $115M; closed December 22, 2025.
- **Focus:** Post-divestiture the company concentrates on the Jack in the Box brand under its "JACK on Track" strategic plan with selective new-market expansion.
- **Digital Surfaces:** Jack app (iOS/Android), Jack Pack loyalty (10 points per $1), mobile/curbside/drive-thru ordering, and DoorDash/Grubhub/Uber Eats delivery integrations.

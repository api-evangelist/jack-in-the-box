# Jack in the Box

Jack in the Box Inc. (NASDAQ: JACK) is a U.S. quick-service restaurant company founded in 1951 and headquartered in San Diego, California. The company operates and franchises approximately 2,200 Jack in the Box hamburger restaurants across 21 states. In December 2021 the company acquired Del Taco for $575M; in October 2025 it agreed to divest Del Taco (~600 locations) to Yadav Enterprises for $115M, with the transaction closing December 22, 2025, refocusing Jack in the Box on its core burger brand. No public developer API is available; all digital ordering, loyalty, and franchise systems are operated via internal platforms and partner integrations.

## Overview

Key Jack in the Box channels and technology surfaces:

- **Jack App** — iOS/Android mobile application for ordering, deals, store locator, and Jack Pack rewards.
- **Jack Pack** — Loyalty program offering points, rewards, birthday offers, and exclusive deals.
- **Mobile Ordering** — In-store pickup, drive-thru, and curbside fulfillment via the app and web.
- **Third-Party Delivery** — DoorDash, Grubhub, and Uber Eats integrations.
- **Franchise Network** — Predominantly franchisee-operated restaurants across the western and southern United States.
- **Investor Relations** — SEC filings, press releases, governance, and sustainability disclosures at investors.jackinthebox.com.

## Corporate Notes

- **Del Taco Divestiture (2025):** Strategic review announced August 2025; sale to Yadav Enterprises announced October 2025 for $115M; closed December 22, 2025.
- **Focus:** Post-divestiture the company concentrates on the Jack in the Box brand with selective new-market expansion (e.g., Chicago, Central Florida).
